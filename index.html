Task 2 — Explain the technique

Look back at the pairs from Task 1. For each one, name the specific word or phrase in the "tell" line that made a claim — then explain, in your own words, how the copy showed it instead of just stating it. Write your answer in the box and check it when you're ready.

Worked example — No. 1 (done for you)

Tell"This skincare product is effective and will make your skin look great."

Show"Imagine a morning where your skin glows elegantly, free of dry patches and irritation. With just one swipe, this serum works overnight to reveal a fresh, youthful glow, so that you may wake up feeling confident, with no makeup needed."

Specific word: effective

How it was converted: By explaining the result of one swipe application within overnight.
